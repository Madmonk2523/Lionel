About Lionel Lubin

I'm an ISSA-Certified Personal Trainer and Nutrition Coach dedicated to helping people transform their bodies and build unstoppable momentum in their fitness journeys.

With over a decade of experience in the fitness industry, I've worked with hundreds of clients ranging from complete beginners to serious athletes. I've learned that successful transformation isn't about extreme diets or overtraining—it's about sustainable systems, consistent effort, and the right guidance.

Credentials & Expertise

  • ISSA Certified Personal Trainer - International Sports Sciences Association
  • ISSA Certified Nutrition Coach
  • Specialties: Strength training, body composition, nutrition coaching, online programming
  • Philosophy: Evidence-based methods, sustainable lifestyle changes, community accountability

My mission is simple: provide world-class coaching that delivers real, measurable results while building a supportive community where everyone can thrive.
